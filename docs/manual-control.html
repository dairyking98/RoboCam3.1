Manual Control

Direct hardware control outside of an experiment — homing, jogging, and troubleshooting.

Workflow guide 1 · Setup 2 · Motion Profiles 3 · Calibration 4 · Experiment 5 · Manual Control 6 · Processing

What's here

  • Home All Axes / Disable Steppers (M18).
  • XY/Z jog pad with preset or custom step size.
  • Go-To by absolute coordinate.
  • Manual Laser ON/OFF toggle.
  • Raw G-code sender with a scrolling response log, for anything not exposed by a dedicated control.

Why this tab exists in the flow

It's not part of the Calibration → Experiment happy path, but it's where you go when something needs correcting outside a run: initial homing after power-on, recovering from an e-stop or a stall, or manually checking the laser wiring before trusting it inside an experiment's Pre/ON/Post phases.

Relative jogging is gated on homed state, same as starting an experiment — the printer's reported position can't be trusted for a relative move until it's been homed at least once.
