What's planned

This tab will expose feed-rate, acceleration, and jerk settings — M203 / M201 / M204 / M205 — for both the Marlin and Klipper backends, letting you tune motion speed and smoothness without dropping to a raw G-code console. It's tracked on the roadmap and not yet implemented.

Until it lands, motion runs at whatever feed-rate/acceleration/jerk the printer's firmware config already has, and any one-off changes can be sent as raw G-code from the Manual Control tab's G-code sender.
